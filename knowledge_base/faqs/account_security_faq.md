# GSR Account & Security FAQ

## Password Reset
Customers can reset their password via the "Forgot Password" link on the
login page, which sends a reset link to their registered email. The reset
link expires after 30 minutes. If a customer says they never received the
email, ask them to check spam/promotions folders before escalating.

## Updating Account Information
Name, email, phone number, and delivery addresses can be updated directly
in Account Settings. Email changes require verification via a confirmation
link sent to the new email address before the change takes effect.

## Suspicious Activity / Account Security
If a customer reports they didn't place an order that appears on their
account, or suspects unauthorized access:
1. Treat this as high priority -- do not just explain policy, escalate to
   a human agent immediately.
2. Advise the customer to change their password immediately as a
   precaution.
3. Do not process any refund or account change for a "disputed" order
   until the security team has reviewed it.

## GSR Prime Membership
Prime membership costs ₹499/year and includes: free shipping on all
orders, priority customer support (faster response times), early access
to sales, and exclusive Prime-only deals. Customers can upgrade to Prime
anytime from Account Settings; downgrades take effect at the end of the
current billing cycle, not immediately.

## Deleting an Account
Account deletion requests must be escalated to a human agent -- this is
not something the support bot can process directly, since it involves
verifying identity and handling any pending orders/refunds first.

## Referral Program
Existing customers get ₹100 GSR Wallet credit for each friend who signs up
using their referral code and completes their first order above ₹500. The
referred friend also gets ₹100 credit on their first order.
