# GSR Warranty Policy

## Which Products Have a Warranty
Warranty coverage applies only to the **electronics** category (smartphones,
laptops, wireless earbuds, smartwatches, Bluetooth speakers). Fashion, home
& kitchen, books, and beauty products are not covered by manufacturer
warranty, though they remain eligible under the standard return policy for
defects found within the return window.

## Warranty Duration
Warranty length depends on the specific product (0, 6, 12, or 24 months as
listed on the product page at time of purchase). The warranty period
starts from the `delivered_date`, not the `order_date`.

## What's Covered
- Manufacturing defects (device fails to power on, battery not holding
  charge under normal use, hardware malfunction)
- Defects present from the time of delivery, discovered during normal use

## What's Not Covered
- Physical damage from drops, water exposure, or misuse
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or third-party accessories
- Normal wear and tear (e.g. minor scratches, battery capacity naturally
  declining over 12+ months of heavy use)
- Software issues unrelated to hardware (these are typically resolved via
  manufacturer software support, not a GSR warranty claim)

## How to File a Warranty Claim
1. Customer describes the issue via chat/support ticket, category:
   `product_question` or a dedicated warranty ticket.
2. If within the warranty period and the issue matches covered symptoms,
   GSR arranges either a repair, replacement, or refund at GSR's discretion
   -- replacement is offered first if stock is available.
3. If the product is out of the standard 7-15 day return window but still
   within warranty, it goes through the warranty claim process (which can
   take longer, typically 7-14 days) rather than the standard fast return
   process.

## Warranty vs. Return -- Which Applies?
- Within the return window (7-10 days for electronics) AND defective ->
  standard return process (faster).
- Outside the return window but within warranty AND defective -> warranty
  claim process.
- Outside both windows, or damage caused by misuse -> not covered; GSR may
  still offer a paid repair as a courtesy, case by case.
