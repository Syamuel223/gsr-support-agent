# GSR Billing & Payment FAQ

## Accepted Payment Methods
UPI, credit card, debit card, net banking, GSR Wallet, and Cash on Delivery
(COD). COD is not available for orders above ₹20,000 or for certain
high-value electronics categories.

## Why Was My Payment Marked "Failed"?
A payment can fail due to: insufficient balance, bank server timeout,
incorrect OTP entry, or the card issuer declining the transaction for
security reasons. If an amount was deducted from the customer's account
but the order still shows `payment failed`, it is typically auto-reversed
by the bank within 5-7 business days. GSR does not hold failed-payment
amounts.

## Double Charge / Duplicate Payment
If a customer reports being charged twice for the same order, this is
usually a temporary hold from the bank rather than an actual double charge.
Advise the customer to check after 24-48 hours; if the duplicate charge is
still visible after that window, escalate to the billing team for a manual
refund of the extra charge.

## Invoice & GST
A digital invoice is generated automatically once an order status changes
to `shipped`, and is available in the customer's order history. GST is
included in the displayed product price for all categories.

## EMI / Installments
EMI options are available on select credit cards for orders above ₹3,000.
EMI eligibility and tenure (3/6/9/12 months) are determined by the
customer's card issuer, not by GSR directly.

## Wallet Credits
GSR Wallet credits (from refunds, promotions, or referrals) do not expire
and can be combined with other payment methods at checkout. Wallet credit
cannot be withdrawn as cash -- it can only be used for GSR purchases.

## Price Match / Price Drop After Purchase
GSR does not offer price-match or price-adjustment refunds if the price of
an item drops after the order is placed. This differs from the
`better_price_found` return reason, which is a valid reason to initiate a
standard return within the applicable return window instead.
