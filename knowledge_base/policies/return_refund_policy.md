# GSR Return & Refund Policy

## General Return Window
Most items can be returned within **7 days of delivery**, unmarked and in
original packaging. The countdown starts from the delivered_date, not the
order_date.

## Category-Specific Return Windows
- **Electronics** (phones, laptops, earbuds, smartwatches, speakers): 10 days
  from delivery. Must include all original accessories and packaging.
- **Fashion** (shoes, clothing, bags): 15 days from delivery. Item must be
  unworn with original tags attached.
- **Home & Kitchen**: 7 days from delivery. Unused, unopened preferred;
  opened items are eligible only if defective.
- **Books**: 7 days from delivery, only if damaged or wrong item received.
  Books are not returnable simply due to change of mind.
- **Beauty & Personal Care**: Returnable only if sealed/unopened, within 7
  days, due to hygiene reasons. Opened items are not eligible for return
  unless defective or wrong item received.

## Non-Returnable Situations
- Item damaged due to customer misuse (not covered as "damaged in transit")
- Return window has expired
- Item missing original tags/packaging where required by category
- Personal care items that have been opened (unless defective)

## Refund Timelines
- **UPI / Net Banking / Wallet**: 2-4 business days after the return is
  received and approved.
- **Credit Card / Debit Card**: 5-7 business days after approval (bank
  processing time varies).
- **Cash on Delivery (COD) orders**: refunded to the customer's bank account
  via a form submitted after approval; 7-10 business days.
- **GSR Wallet Credit** (customer's choice, optional): instant, no waiting
  period.

## Exchange vs Refund
Customers may request either a refund or an exchange for the same item in a
different size/color (fashion only), subject to stock availability. If the
replacement is out of stock, only a refund is offered.

## Return Process
1. Customer requests a return via the app/chat, selecting the reason.
2. GSR schedules a pickup (for most categories) or asks the customer to
   ship the item back using a prepaid label (for low-value items).
3. Once the returned item is received and inspected, the refund/exchange is
   processed.
4. Status updates: `requested` -> `approved` -> `completed` (or `rejected`
   if the item fails inspection, e.g. signs of misuse).

## Return Reasons We Track
`defective_item`, `wrong_item_received`, `no_longer_needed`,
`better_price_found`, `item_damaged_in_transit`, `size_issue`
